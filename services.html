Prevention

Keep Your Vehicle Safe

Proactive measures that significantly reduce the risk of your vehicle being targeted by thieves.

01

Install a Hidden GPS Tracker

A professionally installed, concealed GPS tracker lets you monitor your vehicle's location in real time and receive instant geofence alerts if it moves unexpectedly.

02

Never Leave Keys Unattended

Never leave your keys inside the vehicle, in the ignition, or visible through a window. Thieves often look for easy opportunities before breaking in.

03

Park in Secure, Well-Lit Areas

Always prefer locked garages, gated driveways, or busy well-lit parking lots. Vehicles parked in dark, isolated spots are prime targets for theft.

04

Use Steering Wheel & Pedal Locks

Physical deterrents like steering wheel locks, pedal locks, or gear shift clamps make it visibly difficult for a thief to drive away quickly, often causing them to move on.

05

Close Windows & Lock All Doors

Even a slightly open window or unlocked door is an open invitation. Always double-check that every door and window is fully secured before walking away.

06

Use Dash Cams & Security Cameras

A dash cam with parking mode or a home security camera pointed at your driveway captures crucial footage that can help identify thieves and aid in fast recovery.
